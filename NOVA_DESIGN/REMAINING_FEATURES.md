### Recoverable-vs-fatal distinction + crash isolation  *(7. Error handling)*
- **status:** DONE (2026-06-04) | **effort:** medium

A fatal — panic()/unwrap-on-Err/unwrap_err/failed-assert/integer-overflow/stack-overflow/log_fatal — calls nova_panic, which (when inside a spawned Process) longjmps back to the worker: the worker records exit_status=1 and notifies the Process's monitors, and the WHOLE PROGRAM SURVIVES.
