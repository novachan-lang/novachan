## What's genuinely excellent (credit due, so this doesn't read as pure teardown)

- **Static file serving** with tested defense against a live Windows-junction symlink escape, not just a naive `..` check.
- **Real parameterized queries** everywhere — verified by literally inserting SQL-injection payloads as data and reading them back unharmed.
- **A real Prometheus `/metrics` endpoint** and a real, tested dependency-checking readiness probe DO exist (`forge_obs.nova`) — the problem is the wrong one (`health_route`) is the one that's easy to find.
